# Ethereum (ETH) — overview

Ethereum is a programmable blockchain proposed by Vitalik Buterin in 2013 and launched on 30 July 2015. Its native currency is ether (ETH). Unlike Bitcoin, Ethereum supports general-purpose smart contracts executed by the Ethereum Virtual Machine (EVM), which enabled DeFi, NFTs, stablecoins and DAOs. Эфириум — блокчейн со смарт-контрактами, запущен в 2015 году; идея Виталика Бутерина. Нативная монета — эфир (ETH).

## Consensus: Proof of Stake

On 15 September 2022 Ethereum completed "The Merge", switching from Proof of Work to Proof of Stake (PoS), reducing energy use by about 99.9%. Validators stake 32 ETH to participate in block production and attestation, and can be penalized (slashed) for misbehaviour. Staking yield is roughly in the low single-digit percent per year and varies with network activity. Liquid staking protocols (e.g. Lido, Rocket Pool) issue tokens representing staked ETH. После The Merge (сентябрь 2022) Ethereum работает на Proof of Stake; валидатору нужно 32 ETH.

## Fees, burning and supply

Ethereum has no hard supply cap. Since EIP-1559 (London upgrade, August 2021) a base fee is burned on every transaction, so during periods of high network usage more ETH is burned than issued and supply can shrink (deflationary pressure). Transaction fees are paid in "gas", measured in gwei (1 gwei = 10^-9 ETH). Комиссии (gas) платятся в ETH; по EIP-1559 базовая комиссия сжигается.

## Scaling: Layer 2 and roadmap

Ethereum scales mainly through rollups (Layer 2): Optimistic rollups (Arbitrum, Optimism, Base) and ZK-rollups (zkSync, Starknet, Scroll). The Dencun upgrade (March 2024) introduced "blobs" (EIP-4844) that make rollup data cheaper. The roadmap includes further sharding-like data availability improvements, better validator economics and account abstraction.

## Risks

Competition from other smart-contract platforms (Solana and others), smart-contract bugs and hacks in applications, regulatory scrutiny of staking, and complexity of the multi-layer ecosystem. ETH price is volatile and often correlated with Bitcoin and overall market sentiment.
