# Crypto basics

## Blockchain
A blockchain is a distributed ledger where transactions are grouped into blocks linked by cryptographic hashes. It is maintained by many nodes without a central operator. Блокчейн — распределённый реестр транзакций, защищённый криптографией.

## Proof of Work vs Proof of Stake
Proof of Work (Bitcoin, Litecoin, Dogecoin) secures the network by consuming computing power; miners compete to produce blocks. Proof of Stake (Ethereum, Solana, Cardano) selects validators by the amount of coins they lock as collateral and slashes dishonest ones; it uses far less energy. PoW — безопасность за счёт вычислительной мощности; PoS — за счёт заблокированных монет.

## Coins vs tokens
A coin is the native asset of its own blockchain (BTC, ETH, SOL). A token is created on top of an existing blockchain through smart contracts (for example ERC-20 tokens on Ethereum or SPL tokens on Solana). Монета имеет собственный блокчейн, токен создаётся поверх чужого.

## Stablecoins
Stablecoins aim to track a reference asset, usually USD. Types: fiat-backed (USDT, USDC), crypto-collateralized (DAI) and algorithmic (high-risk; TerraUSD collapsed in May 2022). Risks include reserve transparency, depegging and issuer regulation.

## Wallets and keys
A wallet stores private keys, not coins. The seed phrase (12–24 words) restores all keys; anyone who knows it controls the funds. Hot wallets are connected to the internet; cold (hardware) wallets keep keys offline. Never share your seed phrase or private key. Seed-фраза даёт полный доступ к средствам — никому её не сообщайте.

## Market metrics
Market capitalization = price × circulating supply. Fully diluted valuation (FDV) = price × maximum supply. 24h volume shows trading activity. All-time high (ATH) is the highest historical price. Bitcoin dominance is BTC's share of total crypto market cap. Рыночная капитализация = цена × количество монет в обращении.

## DeFi, NFT, Layer 2
DeFi (decentralized finance) offers lending, trading (DEXs like Uniswap) and yield through smart contracts without intermediaries. NFTs are unique tokens representing ownership of digital items. Layer 2 networks process transactions off the main chain to reduce fees while inheriting its security.

## Common risks and scams
Price volatility, leverage liquidations, exchange insolvency (FTX, 2022), rug pulls, phishing, fake airdrops, and "guaranteed returns" schemes. Always verify contract addresses, use official links and diversify. This knowledge base is educational and is not financial advice. Это не финансовая рекомендация.
