# Solana (SOL) — overview

Solana is a high-performance Layer 1 blockchain designed by Anatoly Yakovenko; the white paper appeared in 2017 and the mainnet beta launched in March 2020. Its native token is SOL, used for transaction fees, staking and securing the network. Солана — высокопроизводительный блокчейн первого уровня, мейннет запущен в марте 2020 года; токен — SOL.

## Technology

Solana combines Proof of Stake with Proof of History (PoH), a cryptographic clock that orders events and helps validators agree on time without heavy communication. Other innovations include Tower BFT consensus, Turbine block propagation, Sealevel parallel transaction execution and Gulf Stream mempool-less forwarding. Smart contracts ("programs") are mostly written in Rust (also C and C++) and run on the Sealevel runtime, not the EVM. Консенсус — PoS + Proof of History; программы пишутся на Rust.

Block time is around 400 milliseconds, and fees are very low (fractions of a cent). Solana is known for high throughput, but it has suffered several network outages and performance degradations, particularly in 2021–2024, which are cited as its main reliability risk. Firedancer, an independent validator client developed by Jump Crypto, aims to improve performance and client diversity.

## Supply and staking

SOL has no hard supply cap; it uses an inflationary schedule that started near 8% per year and decreases by 15% annually toward a long-term rate around 1.5%. A portion of fees is burned. Holders can stake SOL with validators for yield, or use liquid staking tokens such as mSOL and jitoSOL. Инфляция SOL убывает со временем; стейкинг даёт доходность.

## Ecosystem

Solana hosts DeFi (Jupiter, Raydium, Marinade, Kamino), NFTs (Magic Eden), memecoins launched via platforms like pump.fun, and DePIN projects. Its stablecoin activity (USDC, USDT) is significant. Risks: validator centralization concerns, outages, memecoin-driven speculation, regulatory uncertainty and competition from Ethereum L2s.
